Our Story

The Wind that Shaped Us

Kaze — meaning 'wind' or 'breeze' — was born from a vision of Japanese cuisine that breathes. Ever-changing with the seasons, always rooted in tradition. We opened our doors in 2019 with a single promise: that every visit would be different, and every dish, unforgettable.

Chef Hiroshi Tanaka

Executive Chef & Co-founder

Born in Kyoto, trained in Tokyo's finest kaiseki restaurants, Chef Hiroshi brings two decades of mastery to every plate. His philosophy is simple: honour the ingredient, honour the season.
